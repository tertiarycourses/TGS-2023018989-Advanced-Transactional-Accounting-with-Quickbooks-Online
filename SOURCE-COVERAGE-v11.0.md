# Source and Legacy Coverage

Version v11.0 · 16 August 2026

## Legacy deck disposition

The 312-slide v10 deck is the coverage floor. Its useful concepts and scenarios are retained, while click-by-click screen procedures are moved to the Learner Guide and activity packs. The rebuilt deck removes duplicate UI walkthroughs and expands the advanced control, reconciliation, close, reporting, and Singapore-framework depth.

| Legacy coverage | Rebuilt treatment |
|---|---|
| Double entry, COA, customers, suppliers, products, GST setup | Prerequisite bridge + control-ready masters + Activity 1 |
| Invoices, receipts, overdue reminders, statements, estimates, POs, credits, refunds | Advanced order-to-cash and procure-to-pay + Activities 2–3 |
| Inventory and adjustments | Inventory/COGS control + Activity 4 |
| Projects, time, billable expenses | Project profitability and leakage + Activity 5 |
| Bank rules, feeds, reconciliation | Rule governance, match-before-add, reconciliation exceptions + Activities 6–7 |
| Depreciation and recurring transactions | Governed recurring templates and full month-end adjustments + Activity 8 |
| Dashboard, reports, management reports, tags | Report architecture, controlled dimensions, ageing, profitability, variance, cash, management pack + Activity 10 |
| Singapore corporate tax and GST | GST operational controls retained and updated from IRAS; corporate-tax filing detail treated as context rather than a QuickBooks posting workflow |
| GAAP, accrual, standards impact | Reframed to applicable Singapore reporting framework with revenue, inventory, PPE, FX, estimates/errors/policy changes, qualitative characteristics + Activity 12 |

## Basic-course boundary

The supplied `reference/courseware/` package is Financial Analysis for Small and Medium Enterprises, not a basic QuickBooks course. It is used only as supplemental reporting context. Basic QuickBooks navigation and simple invoices/bills/receipts are treated as prerequisites based on the published course page.
